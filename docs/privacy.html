隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 20 日

「夜市翻牌」（以下稱「本遊戲」）是由個人開發者 Kilik 開發的手機遊戲。本政策說明本遊戲會處理哪些資料、用途，以及您可以如何選擇。

1. 本遊戲本身不收集您的個人資料

本遊戲不需要註冊帳號，也不會要求您提供姓名、電話、電子郵件、位置、聯絡人或相片等個人資料。開發者沒有架設伺服器，不會收到或保存您的任何遊戲資料。

2. 儲存在您手機上的資料

以下資料只儲存在您的裝置上，不會傳送給開發者：

  • 遊戲進度：解鎖的夜市、星數、分數、成就、小吃圖鑑、每日任務與簽到紀錄
  • 金幣、道具、裝備、卡背與月卡的剩餘天數
  • 設定：背景音樂、音效的開關與音量、每日提醒的開關
  • 是否購買過「永久移除廣告」

刪除本遊戲時，這些資料會一併刪除，且無法復原。

3. 廣告（Google AdMob）

本遊戲使用 Google 提供的 AdMob 顯示廣告，包含自動出現的插頁廣告，以及您自己選擇觀看以換取遊戲獎勵的獎勵廣告。為了顯示廣告、計算成效與防止詐騙，Google 可能會收集下列資訊：

  • 裝置的廣告識別碼（Android 廣告 ID、iOS 的 IDFA，僅在您允許追蹤時）
  • IP 位址與大略位置（由 IP 推估）
  • 裝置型號、作業系統版本、語言等裝置資訊
  • 廣告的顯示與點擊紀錄、App 的當機與效能資訊

這些資料由 Google 依其政策處理，開發者無法取得可識別您個人的資料。詳情請參考：

  • Google 隱私權政策
  • Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資訊

4. 您可以如何選擇

  • 個人化廣告：所在地區依法需要時，本遊戲會在第一次開啟時詢問您是否同意個人化廣告，之後也可以變更選擇。
  • iPhone：本遊戲會詢問是否允許追蹤；您也可以到「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 追蹤」隨時關閉。
  • Android：可以到系統「設定 → 隱私權 → 廣告」刪除或重設廣告 ID。
  • 永久移除廣告：購買後不再自動跳出插頁廣告；您自己選擇觀看的獎勵廣告仍可使用。
  • 每日提醒：只有在您允許通知後才會開啟。提醒由手機在本機排程，不經過任何伺服器；可以在遊戲的「設定」或系統設定中隨時關閉。
  • 分享成績：成績圖只在您按下「分享成績」時於手機上產生，並交給系統的分享選單，由您選擇要分享到哪裡；開發者不會收到這張圖片。

5. 應用程式內購買

購買金幣、月卡、新手禮包或「永久移除廣告」時，付款完全由 Google Play 或 App Store 處理。開發者不會取得您的信用卡號碼或其他付款資料，只會收到「購買成功」的結果，以便在遊戲中發放內容。

6. 兒童隱私

本遊戲並非專為 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會在知情的情況下收集兒童的個人資料。如果您認為兒童的資料被不當收集，請與我們聯絡。

7. 資料安全

遊戲資料儲存在您的裝置上，受裝置本身的安全機制保護。與 Google 廣告服務之間的連線使用加密傳輸。

8. 政策變更

本政策若有修改，會更新本頁面並修改上方的生效日期。重大變更時，會在遊戲更新說明中告知。

9. 聯絡我們

對本政策或您的資料有任何問題，請寄信至：nightmarketmatch.app@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Night Market Match ("the Game") is a mobile game developed by an independent developer, Kilik. This policy explains what data the Game handles, why, and the choices you have.

1. The Game does not collect your personal information

The Game does not require an account and never asks for your name, phone number, email, location, contacts or photos. The developer runs no servers and does not receive or store any of your game data.

2. Data stored on your device

The following data is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer:

  • Game progress: unlocked night markets, stars, scores, achievements, food collection, daily tasks and check-ins
  • Coins, items, gear, card backs and the remaining days of the monthly pass
  • Settings: music and sound effects on/off and volume, daily reminder on/off
  • Whether "Remove Ads" has been purchased

Uninstalling the Game deletes this data permanently.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, including interstitial ads and rewarded ads that you choose to watch for in-game rewards. To serve ads, measure performance and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID; IDFA on iOS only if you allow tracking)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information such as model, OS version and language
  • Ad impressions and clicks, app crash and performance information

This data is processed by Google under its own policies; the developer cannot access information that identifies you. See:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

4. Your choices

  • Personalized ads: where required by law, the Game asks for your consent to personalized ads the first time you open it, and you can change your choice later.
  • iPhone: the Game asks for permission to track; you can turn it off anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Remove Ads: after purchase, interstitial ads no longer appear; rewarded ads you choose to watch remain available.
  • Daily reminder: only turned on after you allow notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device without any server, and can be turned off anytime in the Game's Settings or your system settings.
  • Sharing your score: the score image is created on your device only when you tap "Share", and handed to the system share sheet so you choose where it goes. The developer never receives it.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases of coins, the monthly pass, the starter pack or "Remove Ads" are processed entirely by Google Play or the App Store. The developer never receives your card number or other payment details, only the result of the purchase so the content can be delivered in the Game.

6. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information has been collected, please contact us.

7. Security

Game data is stored on your device and protected by its built-in security. Connections to Google's ad services are encrypted.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page and the effective date above will be updated. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the Game's release notes.

9. Contact

For any questions about this policy or your data, email: nightmarketmatch.app@gmail.com
